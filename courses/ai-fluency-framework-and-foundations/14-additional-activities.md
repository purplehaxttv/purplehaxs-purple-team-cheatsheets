Self-directed activities page, meant for after the core course is done.

## The point

Self-directed activities for deepening AI competency *after* finishing the course — not required content, a toolkit for continued practice.

## Three activity tracks

1. **Personal AI Fluency Plan** — self-assess across the 4D framework (rate yourself novice/developing/confident per competency), identify development priorities, work with Claude to build an action plan.
2. **Personal Prompt & Pattern Library** — document 5-10 recurring tasks, build reusable prompt templates, organize what's worked. Recommended method: analyze past interactions to identify what made them effective, rather than starting from scratch.
3. **Game Night Exercises** — playful Description/Discernment practice: Swap Riddles (exchange riddles with Claude, focus on reasoning process not just the answer), Cooperative Crosswords, Word Association (practices all three Discernment types — Product, Process, Performance), plus Twenty Questions, collaborative storytelling, role-playing scenarios, and concept-explanation challenges.

## Ties to the 4Ds

Explicitly framed as practical application of all four Ds post-course — the Fluency Plan targets self-assessment across all four, the Prompt Library targets Description specifically, and Game Night targets Description/Discernment.

## Key takeaway to land

The course's own design assumes fluency isn't "complete" at the last lesson — it hands you a self-assessment tool and a practice habit (prompt library, game-night drills) instead of ending cold.

## Exercise: Pick a track

**Why:** turns the course's ending into an ongoing practice habit rather than a stopping point.

Choose one (or more) of the three tracks above and run it for real:
1. Fluency Plan — self-rate all 4 Ds, build an action plan with Claude.
2. Prompt Library — document 5-10 of your own recurring tasks into reusable templates.
3. Game Night — try at least one of the listed games with Claude.
