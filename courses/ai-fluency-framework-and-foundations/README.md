# AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations — Companion Notes

A lesson-by-lesson companion to Anthropic's Claude Academy course *AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations*. Teaches the **4D framework** — Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence — for collaborating with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely. Developed by Rick Dakan (Ringling College of Art and Design) and Joseph Feller (University College Cork) from research on how AI tools were transforming creative and business processes, then built out in partnership with Anthropic (technical/practical content from Maggie Vo and Drew Bent).

Pairs with the [AI Capabilities and Limitations](../ai-capabilities-and-limitations) course — this course covers what the *human* does when collaborating with AI; that one covers what the *model* is doing under the hood.

## What you'll learn

- What AI Fluency means and why it matters for effective, efficient, ethical, and safe AI collaboration
- The 4D framework applied to real AI interactions
- **Automation, Augmentation, and Agency** — three distinct ways of engaging with AI
- How generative AI and LLMs work: key capabilities and limitations
- **Delegation** — strategic decisions via Problem Awareness, Platform Awareness, and Task Delegation
- **Description** — clear communication via Product, Process, and Performance Description, plus foundational prompting technique
- **Discernment** — critically evaluating AI outputs, process, and behavior; refining through the Description-Discernment loop
- **Diligence** — Creation, Transparency, and Deployment Diligence as ways of taking responsibility for AI-assisted work

## Course arc

1. **Framing** — Introduction, then "Why do we need AI Fluency?" and "The 4D Framework": Automation vs. Augmentation vs. Agency as three modes of engagement, then naming the 4Ds.
2. **Deep Dive 1: generative AI** — fundamentals + capabilities/limitations (strongest tie-in point to the companion course — cross-reference directly here).
3. **Delegation** — closer look, then project planning and delegation practice.
4. **Description** — closer look, then Deep Dive 2 on effective prompting techniques.
5. **Discernment** — closer look, then the Description-Discernment loop as the refinement cycle tying the two competencies together.
6. **Diligence** — closer look; taking responsibility for AI-assisted work.
7. **Conclusion & practice** — conclusion, additional activities.
