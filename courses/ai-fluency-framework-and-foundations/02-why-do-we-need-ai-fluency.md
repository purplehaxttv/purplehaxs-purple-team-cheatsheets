## The point

Having access to AI is not the same as being fluent with it. The gap shows up in familiar moments: an unexpected AI response you don't know how to handle, struggling to explain what you actually need, wondering if your shared information is protected. AI Fluency exists to close that specific gap — not to make you a technical expert or hand you a list of trending prompts.

## Three ways people engage with AI

- **Automation** — AI completes a specific task based on your instructions (summarize a document, draft an email, plan an itinerary). You define what's needed; the AI executes. Works well with a clear outcome in mind; harder when you're not yet sure what you're looking for.
- **Augmentation** — you and the AI collaborate as a creative thinking/problem-solving partnership, not a task-automation machine. Example: developing a stuck story character through back-and-forth conversation, or working through a hard architectural problem. The AI "doesn't do work for you, but helps you do your work better." Best when solutions aren't straightforward and you need room to explore.
- **Agency** — AI works independently on your behalf. Example: an assistant that categorizes incoming emails and drafts responses to urgent ones, or an interactive character/experience that runs on its own. The key shift: rather than defining specific actions, **you establish the AI's knowledge and behavior patterns** — "less like a script writer giving exact directions and more like a director setting a vision."

None of the three is inherently better — they serve different situations, and a single project might use all three. Many people start with Automation, but Augmentation and Agency take fuller advantage of AI's actual capabilities and often produce the most creative/effective outcomes.

## Ties to the 4Ds

This lesson doesn't detail the 4Ds (that's [The 4D Framework](./03-the-4d-framework.md) next) — it establishes *why* they're needed: whichever of the three modes you're in, the same four competencies determine whether the collaboration goes well.

## Key takeaway to land

"Moving beyond prompt tricks" isn't just a slogan — it's the reason three *different* modes of engaging AI exist at all. Fluency means recognizing which mode a task calls for (Automation, Augmentation, or Agency), not defaulting to one because it's the one you learned first.
